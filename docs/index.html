FAILURE MODES, MADE EXPLICIT

The form can look right
while the data is wrong.

01

Food identity

Checks the linked food object against the intended name or stable ID. Near matches fail closed.

02

Structured rows

Keeps quantity, unit, food, and note in their own fields. No duplicated display strings.

03

Method coverage

Flags ingredients that never appear in the method and method references absent from the list.

04

Duplicate recipes

Detects reordered names and overlapping ingredient sets before they become separate recipes.

05

Plan arithmetic

Reports meal occurrences, total servings, and unique recipes as different numbers.

06

Read-after-write

Re-reads saved data and executes the category, tag, tool, and food filters users depend on.
